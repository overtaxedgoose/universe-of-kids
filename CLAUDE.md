# universe-of-kids

Static ARCHIVE of The Universe of Kids (2018-2025), a closed print-on-demand kids' shop —
one of Jajeh Group's "Past & Private Ventures". Captured from its Squarespace site
(giraffe-toucan-dap6.squarespace.com) in August 2026 before winding down Squarespace.

- Hosted as a PROJECT page: https://overtaxedgoose.github.io/universe-of-kids/ (no custom
  domain, no CNAME). All paths are RELATIVE (./...) because of the subpath — keep them that way.
- The shop is display-only (14 products with images, prices, descriptions from the original
  JSON-LD). Do not add purchase functionality; the business is closed.
- noindex meta is deliberate — it's an archive, not a live property.
- Full raw capture (all HTML + 175 images) was taken at archive time; product mockups here
  are the primary product images only.
