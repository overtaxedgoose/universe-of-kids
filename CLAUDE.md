# universe-of-kids

Brand home for The Universe of Kids — designs made by kids, for kids. One of Jajeh Group's ventures.
Ran 2018-2025 as a Squarespace + Printful print-on-demand shop, archived to this repo in August 2026,
and RELAUNCHING in late 2026.

- Hosted on GitHub Pages at https://theuniverseofkids.com/ (CNAME file in repo; DNS on Squarespace
  nameservers, A records + www CNAME -> GitHub). The old project-page URL
  overtaxedgoose.github.io/universe-of-kids/ 301s here. All paths are RELATIVE (./...) — keep them that way.
- `index.html` — home: story, Shop button, Instagram (@theuniverseofkids).
- `shop.html` — "Past Designs" gallery of the 14 original products (images + names only; no prices,
  the business does not sell from this site).
- The actual store is Shopify Starter + Printful (Patrick's existing Printful account) at
  https://shop.theuniverseofkids.com/ (CNAME `shop` -> shops.myshopify.com, added at Squarespace DNS).
  Shop links on this site point there. Do NOT build cart/checkout here.
- The kids design and choose what to sell; t-shirts were the best sellers.
- Original Tailor Brands logo packs and a 2019 sales dashboard live in Drive under
  The Universe of Kids / TUOK. Full raw Squarespace capture (all HTML + 175 images) is
  raw-capture-2026-08.tar.gz in this repo.
